for my Anny with all my love

my Anny,

I miss you. Not in the dramatic, poetic way I’m about to pretend — in the very real, very annoying way where I keep thinking of things to tell you and then remember you’re not right here to hear them.

I miss your voice. I miss the way a normal conversation with you turns into something I’m still thinking about hours later. I miss knowing what your day sounds like. I miss the version of the day where you’re in it.

So I made this instead of just sitting here being dramatic about it. A letter, some games, a few reminders, and a whole machine dedicated to stealing you away for a day. It’s not the same as having you here. It’s just my way of saying you’re on my mind more than a reasonable person should be.

You don’t have to do anything with any of this. Just know that you are missed. Loudly. From wherever I am. Always.

Now come online. Please. I have so much to say.

all my love, always ♥

xoxo
